- Published title (spelling retained): **Tesla Roadtser 2026**
- Creator: **asiphy007**
- Original: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/tesla-roadtser-2026-7630c9fe842d4f5ea86fc1f122753b89
- Creator: https://sketchfab.com/asiphy007
- License: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**

- Public distribution mirror used: https://github.com/codinguniversefromEric/roadster/blob/main/car.glb
- Original mirror download: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codinguniversefromEric/roadster/main/car.glb
- Checked: 22 October 2026.

The creator labels the asset as AI-generated. This is a community interpretation of the 2026 reveal vehicle, not Tesla CAD or an official manufacturer asset. It is used for every player and AI roadster. No endorsement is implied.

**Modifications:** source atlas sampled into linear vertex colours; nearby vertices welded; three mesh detail levels prepared (approximately 36,000 / 12,000 / 3,500 triangles); unused resources removed; Draco-compressed; recalculated shading normals; scaled and centered for gameplay; body paint mask, team colours, alternate material finishes, lighting, light bars, underglow and rocket-port effects added. Added lights and effects are not claims about the real vehicle's dimensions or performance.

Files: `assets/roadster-2026.glb`, `assets/roadster-game.glb`, `assets/roadster-far.glb`.
